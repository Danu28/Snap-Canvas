SnapCanvas Help

Capture → Annotate → Export. All PNG, lossless.

  • Tools: Rectangle (R) / Arrow (A) / Text (T) / Select (S) / Blur (B) / Pixelate (P)
  • Select: click to select, drag to move, handles to resize, arrows to nudge
  • Text: click canvas, type, Enter to place, Shift+Enter newline
  • Zoom: Fit (0) / − / + or click % to reset; Space+drag or middle-drag to pan
  • History: Undo Ctrl+Z, Redo Ctrl+Shift+Z, Duplicate Ctrl+D, Delete Del, Esc deselect
  • Crop: Select a rectangle/redaction, then Crop to Selection
  • Capture: Ctrl+Shift+1 Full · 2 Visible · 3 Selected
